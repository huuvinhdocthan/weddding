🌹 Our Love Story

Our Story

💫

The First Meeting

The venue: a countryside "Casino" during Lunar New Year. The groom ruthlessly won every last bit of the bride's lucky money. With nothing left to bet, the bride had no choice but to "claim" the groom as her husband, so his salary could pay off the debt.

💌

The "Payback" Era

Date after date after date. The bride's real mission: drain the groom's wallet to recover her Tết losses. The result: the groom went completely bankrupt, surrendered willingly, and begged to take care of the bride for free, for life.

💍

The Proposal - Cards on the Table

A warm evening on a breezy beach. The groom gave the bride a diamond ring bought from the one and only Ms. Phương Hằng and proposed a "shared home" deal. And so the card game from that year officially ends with a happy wedding: two rival players, now one family!

👰🤵

Under One Roof

24 - 25.10.2026 - the day Yến Thi & Hữu Vinh officially become husband and wife. The chapter that began with a losing hand at Tết closes, and a new journey begins: "One home, together!"
